# Paperweight 1.1.0

What's new since 1.0.0.

Still free, no cloud attached
Paperweight has zero monetization plan, doesn't talk to the cloud so it will never report back your usage. I made this for my spouse, but if you find some use for it and appreciate it, leaving a review is thanks enough.

Days off and quiet days
Plan exceptions to your weekly schedule: a single day or a run of days that is open all day, quiet all day, or treated like another weekday. Find them under Restricted apps on the Home screen, or in Settings while locked. The same safety rule applies: a quiet day can start today, a day off waits for tomorrow.

The week ahead
The Home screen now shows the next seven days from today, with a dot on any day you have planned, so a long weekend or a work trip is visible before it arrives.

Enforcement fixes
Fixed several ways Paperweight could drift out of its schedule: a window that ran to midnight could stay open until the morning, a timed unlock could outlast its countdown while the app was in the background, and a schedule change waiting for tomorrow had no boundaries of its own. Apps now go quiet when they should, no need to periodically open the app.

Widget
The countdown keeps ticking across an open-to-quiet boundary, and past a day away it speaks in days: "2½ days", not "63h 44m". The open state now says Open, matching the rest of the app.

A new icon
Liquid Glass icon style on iOS 26 and drawn the same way in the widgets. It now adapts to light, dark, and tinted Home Screens.

Also:
Painting the schedule across the current hour no longer shifts the grid under your finger.
